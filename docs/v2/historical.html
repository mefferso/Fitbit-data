Comparing the comeback fairly

Easy, heart-rate-controlled 2026 efforts are not directly comparable to an all-out mile or race-like 2011 performance. Age-graded race percentages require an explicit standard and comparable event distance; they are not guessed here.

Recent evidence: Oct 11, 2026: 3.16 miles at 12:41/mi, average HR 141, deliberately regulated effort. The goal remains a sustainable 26:25 5K—not trying to run yesterday's personal best tomorrow.

Import design: keep source, source-row ID, recorded date, distance, time, and QC flags. Future Runkeeper data can be added separately, with potential cross-app duplicates flagged before any totals are combined. Original and modern physiological metrics remain in separate datasets.
