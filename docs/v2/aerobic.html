    How daytime HRV is measured

    RMSSD is the root mean square of differences between successive normal beat intervals. FIT estimates use screened adjacent RR intervals in a continuous window of about five minutes. Activity timestamps label the session start. The FIT HRV messages themselves lack timestamps, and sensor origin can be unknown.

    Health Snapshot is an approximately two-minute measurement. Enter Garmin’s RMSSD and optional SDRR, which measures the spread of the recorded RR intervals. SDRR is shown separately from RMSSD.

    Nighttime HRV is measured during sleep. Activity, Snapshot, and overnight readings each need their own comparison conditions. Garmin stress is a derived score. Body Battery combines several signals into an energy estimate. Both have different units and meaning from HRV in milliseconds.
